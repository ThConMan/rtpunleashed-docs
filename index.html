HOW IT WORKS

The waiting is the search

The ordering is the whole trick. The expensive part finishes while the player is still watching the animation, so the teleport itself costs nothing at the moment it happens.

01

The charge-up begins

A helix, vortex or ring animation winds up with pitch-ramped sounds and a countdown title. Move or take a hit and it cancels cleanly, costing nothing.

02

Chunks load in the background

Candidate points are sampled from the ring and rejected against the world border before a single chunk is touched. Each chunk that does load is checked in several places before another is paid for.

03

Zero, and you are there

Departure burst, arrival chime, and a few seconds of protection so nobody shoots you on landing — dropped early if you swing first. Only now do the cooldown and the fee apply.

/rtp A blocked teleport refunds the fee and clears the cooldown. Players are never charged for a landing that did not happen.

THE PICKER

A menu you arrange by clicking

World icons space themselves out symmetrically instead of packing shoulder to shoulder. Prefer your own arrangement? Pick a world up in the layout editor and click the slot it should live in — that pins it, and everything unpinned keeps flowing around your pins.

Menu height, the filler blocks in the empty slots, every label and every lore line are yours to set, in game, without touching a file.

Every window is decoration only. Clicks, shift-clicks, drags, hotbar and offhand swaps and hopper pulls are all refused, and menus force-close on reload so no window outlives its listener.

Wilderness Teleport

Two worlds land at columns three and seven, mirrored around the centre. One sits dead centre; five per row still keep a gap between neighbours. Each world picks its own icon — these are the environment defaults.

FEATURES

Built for servers that actually have players on

Every decision here started as a complaint about some other RTP plugin.

NO RESTARTS

Configured entirely in game

Radii, shapes, cooldowns, warmups, costs, Y ranges, scan modes, and adding or removing worlds. Every change applies instantly and writes itself back to disk. Worlds are opt-in, so nothing routes players anywhere until you say so.

LANDINGS

Somewhere sensible, every time

Lava, cactus, fire, powder snow, berry bushes and cobwebs are refused. Oceans and rivers are skipped. Water and leaf landings are opt-in, and roofed worlds get a volume scan instead of a heightmap guess that would drop players on the nether roof.

0

Dupe vectors

Menus hold rendered decoration, never real items, behind a guard that refuses every inventory mutation at lowest priority.

8

Columns per chunk

Chunk loads are the real cost, so each one is searched in several places before another is paid for. Generation can be switched off per world.

3

Charge animations

Helix, vortex and rings, each with configurable particles, density and pitch-ramped sounds. Or none, if your server prefers quiet.

BEDROCK

The same plugin on both clients

Chest menus, chat prompts for typed values, plain sounds and particles. No anvil renames or sign editors for Geyser to mistranslate, so what a Java player sees, a Bedrock player sees.

OPTIONAL HOOKS

Vault and PlaceholderAPI

Per-world costs charged on arrival when Vault is installed, and placeholders for cooldowns, playtime and world counts when PlaceholderAPI is. The plugin loads and runs without either.

PLAYTIME PERKS

Regulars wait less

Set thresholds and the cooldown shortens as people play. Read straight from the vanilla play-time statistic, so there is nothing to grant, nothing to sync, and no rank ladder to keep in step with your permissions plugin.

TEXT

MiniMessage, everywhere

Every message, world name, menu title and lore line takes the full MiniMessage surface: hex, gradients, rainbow, bold, fonts, hover and click. Blank a key and that message goes silent or that lore line disappears.

A world's display name renders wherever it appears — chat, the action bar, the picker, the arrival message — so one setting styles it everywhere.

Text a player controls is deliberately never parsed, so nobody can inject formatting or a fake click event through a name.

COMMANDS

Short to type, hard to misuse

Aliases: /wild, /randomtp, /rtpu.

/rtp

Teleport, or open the picker when several worlds are available.

/rtp <world>

Go straight to one world, skipping the menu.

/rtp <player>

Send someone else. Console and command blocks welcome.

/rtp cancel

Stop a charging teleport before it lands.

/rtp info

Effective settings, your playtime and your cooldown.

/rtp admin

The live configuration menu. Everything is in here.

PER WORLD

A defaults layer, and overrides only where you need them

Each world inherits the defaults and changes just what it should. The admin menu tells you which layer answered for every setting.

RTP Admin - main

The real controls, and the real items behind them. Left and right click nudge a value, shift moves it five steps, and the drop key takes an exact number typed in chat — which is also why it works on Bedrock.

Shape

A circle or a square ring, anchored on world spawn, the world border centre, or fixed coordinates.

Radius

Minimum and maximum distance from that anchor. The world border is respected with a margin so nobody lands inside the wall.

Height

A Y band the search will accept, plus surface, volume, or automatic scanning per world.

Timing

Warmup, cooldown and the arrival protection window, each per world and each shortened by playtime.

Cost

Charged through Vault on arrival, if you use it. Multiplied down for long-serving players.

Redirects

Send a nether or lobby /rtp into the overworld instead, with target-world.

Blacklists

Unsafe ground, unsafe headroom and blocked biomes, all with * wildcards.

Get RTP Unleashed

  1. Drop the jar into plugins/ and start the server.
  2. Run /rtp admin and click a world to enable it. Worlds are opt-in.
  3. Set the radius and cooldown to taste. There is no step four.
