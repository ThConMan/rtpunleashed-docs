Wilderness Teleport

Icons space themselves out automatically — or pin any world to any slot by clicking. Menu height, filler blocks and every label are yours.

Built for servers that actually have players on

Every decision here started as a complaint about some other RTP plugin.

The search runs during the charge-up

Chunks load asynchronously, chained one at a time, while the countdown plays. By the time it hits zero the destination is usually already found, so the jump is instant.

Configured entirely in game

Radii, shapes, cooldowns, warmups, costs, Y ranges, scan modes, world add and remove. Every change applies instantly and writes itself back to disk. No restarts, no file editing.

Menus that cannot dupe

Every window is decoration only, behind a guard that refuses clicks, shift-clicks, drags, hotbar and offhand swaps and hopper pulls — and force-closes on reload so no window outlives its listener.

Perks earned by playtime

Set thresholds like 10h or 7d and regulars get shorter cooldowns, faster warmups and cheaper teleports. Read from the vanilla statistic, so there is nothing to grant and nothing to sync.

Land somewhere sensible

Lava, cactus, fire, powder snow, berry bushes and cobwebs are refused. Oceans and rivers are skipped. Water and leaf landings are opt-in, and roofed worlds get a volume scan instead of a heightmap guess.

MiniMessage everywhere

Gradients, hex, rainbow, fonts, hover and click across every message, world name, menu title and lore line. Player-supplied text is never parsed, so nobody can inject formatting through a name.

Bedrock players get the same thing

Chest menus, chat prompts for typed values, plain sounds and particles — no anvil renames or sign editors that Geyser mistranslates. What a Java player sees, a Bedrock player sees.

What happens when a player types the command

The ordering is the whole trick: the expensive part finishes while the player is still watching the animation.

1

Eligibility, once

Permission, world enabled, cooldown, balance. A refusal happens immediately and costs nothing.

2

The charge-up starts and the hunt begins

A helix, vortex or ring animation winds up with pitch-ramped sounds while the async search samples the ring, rejects anything outside the world border, and loads candidate chunks one at a time.

3

Several columns per loaded chunk

Chunk loads are the real cost, so each one is checked in several places before another is paid for. Chunk generation can be switched off per world for pregenerated maps.

4

The countdown ends and you are there

Departure burst, arrival chime, and a few seconds of protection so you are not shot on landing — dropped early if you swing first.

5

Only now does it cost anything

The cooldown starts and the fee is taken on a successful landing. A blocked teleport refunds and clears the cooldown.

Commands

Aliases: /wild, /randomtp, /rtpu.

/rtpTeleport, or open the picker when several worlds are available
/rtp <world>Go to a specific world
/rtp <player>Send someone else. Console and command blocks welcome
/rtp guiThe world picker
/rtp cancelStop a charging teleport
/rtp infoEffective settings, your playtime and your cooldown
/rtp adminThe live configuration menu
/rtp reloadRe-read the files after hand edits

Per world, and all of it live

Each world inherits a defaults layer and overrides only what it needs. The admin menu shows which layer answered.
